A few questions

Each has a single best numerical answer. Take your time.

1. A bat and a ball cost £1.10 in total. The bat costs £1.00 more than the ball. How much does the ball cost, in pence?
2. If it takes 5 machines 5 minutes to make 5 widgets, how long would it take 100 machines to make 100 widgets, in minutes?
3. In a lake, there is a patch of lily pads. Every day, the patch doubles in size. If it takes 48 days for the patch to cover the entire lake, how long would it take to cover half the lake, in days?

About this study

This study looks at whether performance on the catching game relates to scores on a short reasoning task. We recorded which falling objects you caught and missed during the game, along with your answers to the three questions that followed.

About the reasoning questions

The three questions are the Cognitive Reflection Test (Frederick, 2005). Each has an intuitively appealing but incorrect answer. For reference: the ball costs 5p, not 10p; the machines take 5 minutes, not 100; the lily pads cover half the lake on day 47, not day 24.
